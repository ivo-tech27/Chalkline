College Football Playoff scenarios · 2026 season · 12 teams

Chalkline

Move teams, crown conference champions, log scores and pick the bracket.

Add to Home Screen
In Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen.” Chalkline opens full screen like an app.

How the field is built. The five highest-ranked conference champions get automatic bids; the next seven highest-ranked teams get at-large bids. All 12 are seeded straight by ranking, and seeds 1–4 get first-round byes. Seeds 5–8 host first-round games on campus.

Win chances come from a simple model based on poll position and record, not betting lines. Your edits, picks, scores and team choice save in this browser only. When a new poll arrives, the app starts fresh from it and keeps your team choice.
