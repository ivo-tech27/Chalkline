In Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen.” Chalkline opens full screen like an app.

How the field is built. The five highest-ranked conference champions get automatic bids; the next seven highest-ranked teams get at-large bids. All 12 are seeded straight by ranking, and seeds 1–4 get first-round byes. Seeds 5–8 host first-round games on campus.

Rankings come only from the College Football Playoff selection committee. Win chances come from a simple model based on committee position and record, not betting lines; the Chalk–Chaos slider loosens it up. Your edits, picks and team choice save on this device only. When the committee releases a new ranking, the app starts fresh from it and keeps your team choice.
